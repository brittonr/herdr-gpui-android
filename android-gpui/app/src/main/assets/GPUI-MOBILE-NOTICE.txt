SurfaceView host; InputProxy callbacks are routed through a protected gpuiIme
hook with documented comments. The app overrides that hook to keep committed
IME and physical input in one ordered, session-fenced FIFO. Android InputConnection
composition mechanics are retained.
This integration uses gpui-pre and gpui-pre-wgpu 0.3.7 without changing the desktop app's pinned 0.3.6.
